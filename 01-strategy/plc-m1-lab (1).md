## Write your one-page strategy
- **Winning aspiration: define winning in the customer's terms, not internal metrics. What does success look like for the specific person you serve?:** Help superintendents and foremen resolve jobsite blockers without chasing people through group texts or re-entering updates after their shift. They should know who owns a problem, what happens next, and whether work can proceed or is it blocked.
- **Where to play: segment, geography, and use case, explicitly. Which customers, markets, and surfaces will you focus on?:** US exclusively, Superintendents and foremen on active commercial construction projects within existing enterprise general-contractor accounts. Surface: SImplle anf UI oprimised mobile workflow, connected to the existing Merifian proejct work software. Use cases: Capture, route, and resolve everyday field blockers requiring coordination with the backoffice.
- **How to win: your differentiator. What can you do that your specific competitors cannot easily replicate?:** Turn a phone photo into an accountable resolution loop: capture a blocker, identify its location, route it to an owner, and return a clear answer to the field. All püowered by AI. Meridian can connect reports to enterprise workflows already used by the office - this may be the point where competitors can not easily replicate.

What will you build, buy, or partner for?:** Reliable mobile capture with offline support and minimal typing, Routing and resolution connected to enterprise records without duplicate entry, Role-based views that show field teams only the information they need., Partner up with the light-house customers. Include interviews with people who stopped using the workflow. Assign a product lead to each pilot. Jointly review unanswered reports, routing failures, and administrative burden.

## Name your one hard no
- **Your one hard no:** Replacing every conversation would broaden the scope and create another inbox. We will support comments and clarification within a blocker, but remain focused on accountable resolution.

## Write your three OKRs
- **Objective:** Make Meridian the trusted way for pilot field teams to get everyday jobsite blockers resolved within the next 90 days.
- **KR1:** Increase the share of eligible field leaders meaningfully using the blocker workflow in at least three of the preceding four weeks from 15% to 60% by day 90.
- **KR2:** Reduce median elapsed time from reporting to field-confirmed resolution for comparable routine blockers from 24 hours to 12 hours by day 90.
- **KR3:** Increase the share of sampled blocker cases resolved without repeating the report through another channel or re-entering it into Meridian from 25% to 70% by day 90.

## AI pressure-test
- **Which challenge from the AI is most valid, and why?:** KR2 becomes unachievable no matter how good the mobile capture UX is, which means the 90-day objective fails on its own terms. The differentiation story in "how to win" stops mattering, because you'd be winning the wrong race — a faster, prettier way to put a request into a queue that was never the constraint. And the moat question becomes academic, because there's no point worrying about competitors copying a capture-and-routing loop that doesn't actually move the metric it's supposed to move.
- **What would you change based on the pushback, and what would you defend?:** I'd restate KR2 as something like: increase the share of comparable routine blockers resolved within 12 hours from [baseline]% to [target]%.
